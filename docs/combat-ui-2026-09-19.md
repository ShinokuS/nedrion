# Combat, loot interaction and orb HUD

## HUD and art
- Replaced the small lower-left panel and upper-left health bar with a centered gothic frame. Red sphere = health; violet sphere = level experience. No unimplemented mana resource is shown.
- `public/assets/gothic-hud.png` is an original imagegen-created transparent frame inspired by the supplied reference; liquid levels are rendered and cropped dynamically by the game.
- All 13 skill/support icons are imported from the supplied Hero Siege Talent sprites with source provenance in the asset manifest. Automatic skill icons are separate from Q/R/F active slots.
- Active slots accept only healing and blink; stale automatic assignments are cleared.

## Combat
- Boss hit tint now expires and preserves sprite details. Every living boss gets its own reusable health row; rebuilding the regular HUD does not duplicate boss rows.
- Native boss locomotion/cast sequences remain enabled. Reaper scythes now play all four source frames. Bosses pause movement during their cast wind-up.
- Meteor effects now fall along a descending arc with a native fire trail and Pyromancer explosion. Multishot spawns additional meteors across nearby targets. Rain and orbiting swords also use multishot; duration extends supported effects; haste changes cooldowns; pierce applies to projectile skills.
- Supports traverse the actual connected socket group. Broken links and removed supports immediately remove their modifiers from subsequent casts, including accumulated level upgrades. Unsupported combinations do not offer meaningless level choices.
- Slower spawn-pressure growth, lower normal-enemy health/contact-damage growth, a 160-enemy cap and reduced wave pressure during boss encounters. Local spatial-hash separation reduces overlapping enemy stacks while respecting the same terrain collisions as navigation.
- Removed the unowned gold marker created when enemies dropped a container. Experience drops remain visible as native collectible orbs.

## Inventory and looting
- Alt reveals/tints visible ground items and searchable containers.
- Left click on a socket without a held gem forwards to the item's full hit target. Right click still extracts an installed gem. Ordinary double clicks no longer trigger unsolicited transfers.
- Ctrl + right click transfers revealed loot; right click equips directly from a chest or stash. Dragging to an equipment slot also supports these sources. Equipment swaps are atomic and preserve container discovery boundaries.
- The free merchant replenishes missing catalogue entries on purchase/reopening.
- Inspect/smoke pages no longer write gameplay saves.

## Checks and scope
28 Node tests, including 100 procedural map seeds; 25 browser integration tests, including boss animation frame changes, temporary hit tint, simultaneous health rows, meteor multishot, Alt visibility, socket clicks and direct chest equipment. Visual checks at 1280×720 verified the orb HUD, active icons and two boss bars. The enemy tuning is a new prototype baseline, not a claim of final balance or complete reproduction of Hero Siege boss AI.
